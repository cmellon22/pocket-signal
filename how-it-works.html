BEHIND THE SIGNAL

How Pocket Signal works

Pocket Signal combines multiple predictive models to turn NFL matchup data into an easy-to-read forecast.

THE IDEA

One matchup. Multiple perspectives.

Instead of asking one model to predict everything, Pocket Signal uses separate models for different parts of the game.

The result is a forecast that shows who is more likely to win, the expected scoring margin, and the expected total points.

FORECAST ENGINE

Three core predictions

Each model answers a different question about the game.

01
WINNER MODEL

Who is more likely to win?

The winner model estimates each team's probability of winning the game.

Example BAL — 79.0% win probability
02
MARGIN MODEL

By how much?

The margin model estimates the expected point difference between the two teams.

Example BAL by 8.2
03
TOTAL MODEL

How many points?

The total model estimates the combined scoring output expected from both teams.

Example Projected total — 51.2
PROJECTED SCORE

Turning the forecast into a score

Pocket Signal uses the projected margin and projected total together to produce a coherent final-score projection.

This keeps the displayed score consistent with both the margin and total forecasts.

CONFIDENCE

Signal strength

Signal strength represents confidence in the winner forecast — not a guarantee of the result.

Strong 70%+

The winner model sees a larger separation between the two teams.

Medium 60–69.9%

Pocket Signal has a clear preference, but there is still meaningful uncertainty.

Weak Below 60%

The matchup is closer and the winner forecast is more uncertain.

MODEL DISAGREEMENT

What happens when the models disagree?

The winner and margin forecasts are produced by separate models, so close games can occasionally produce different answers.

Pocket Signal does not force the models to agree. The disagreement stays visible so users can see the additional uncertainty in the matchup.

MODEL DISAGREEMENT
Winner model NYG 60.5%
Margin model NO by 1.3

Historically, the winner model has been slightly more accurate at selecting the outright winner, but the difference is small.

ACCOUNTABILITY

Every forecast stays on the record

Pocket Signal preserves its original pregame forecasts before the result is known.

After the game, those forecasts are graded for winner accuracy, margin error and total error on the Track Record page.
