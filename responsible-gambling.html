Pocket Signal offers sports information, not guaranteed outcomes. No model, market gap, confidence label or public record removes uncertainty. Fantasy and wagering decisions remain your responsibility.

If you choose to wager

Meet the legal age and eligibility requirements in your location. Set time and spending limits in advance, never use money needed for essentials, and do not chase losses or treat betting as a source of income. Take a break or stop if gambling affects your finances, relationships or wellbeing.

Support in the United States

The National Council on Problem Gambling offers information and referrals for people concerned about their own or someone else's gambling. Call or text 1-800-MY-RESET (1-800-697-3738), or visit ncpgambling.org/help-treatment for support and chat options. This is a gambling-support resource, not an emergency service.

Outside the United States

Consult a qualified local support provider or your jurisdiction's official gambling-help resources. The appropriate service and eligibility rules vary by location.
